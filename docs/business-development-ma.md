# BizCrax36 — Business Development, Partnerships and M&A

## Opportunity categories
Organic growth, B2B lead generation, vendor registration, subcontracting, authorized distribution, reseller/channel partnerships, licensing, franchise evaluation, joint ventures, strategic alliances, minority investments, acquisition, merger, succession and divestiture.

## Required evidence
Each lead requires source URL, publication/retrieval dates, verified legal entity, named counterparty where public, current opportunity status, eligibility, geographic scope, value and currency (or undisclosed), contact route, conflicts of interest, and an evidence-confidence grade. Never infer a partnership invitation from an investment announcement. Never label a business for sale without direct authorized evidence.

## Collaboration score (0–100)
Demand evidence 20; strategic fit 20; capabilities 15; counterparty verification 15; financial viability 15; execution/legal risk 15. Missing material evidence caps status at RESEARCH_REQUIRED; do not rank as verified.

## M&A diligence gate
1. Confirm authorized sale or merger mandate and beneficial ownership.
2. Check financial statements, cash flow, customer concentration, debts, tax and litigation.
3. Review contracts, IP, employment, regulatory permissions and data privacy.
4. Model standalone value, synergies, integration costs and downside cases.
5. Require independent legal, tax and accounting review before transactions.
