# BizCrax36 International Opportunities — Research & Entry Plan

## Coverage
Any country and any nonnegative project value, including undisclosed amounts. Public tenders, private RFQs, supplier onboarding, partnerships, subcontracting, and investments are distinct types and must not be conflated.

## Initial source registry (official portals; availability and individual listings not yet verified)
- UNGM: https://www.ungm.org/Public/Notice
- EU TED: https://ted.europa.eu/
- World Bank: https://projects.worldbank.org/en/projects-operations/opportunities
- Asian Development Bank: https://www.adb.org/projects/tenders
- US SAM.gov: https://sam.gov/
- Australia AusTender: https://www.tenders.gov.au/
- Singapore GeBIZ: https://www.gebiz.gov.sg/

## Priority entry hypotheses
1. **ASEAN**: digital workflow services, industrial maintenance, spare parts and training; investigate cross-border supplier registration and partner networks.
2. **Middle East**: facility operations, technical training, energy and industrial supply; investigate local registration and sponsorship requirements by country.
3. **Europe**: software and specialized engineering services; evaluate procurement eligibility, privacy and product certification.
4. **Australia/North America**: remote software, technical documentation and niche supply; assess insurance, taxes, local content and vendor onboarding.
5. **Africa/South Asia**: development-financed procurement, infrastructure and training; verify payment security and counterparty risk.

## Mandatory detailed record
Country, issuer, title, procurement type, notice reference, original issuer URL, posted date, closing time and timezone, currency, disclosed/undisclosed value, scope and deliverables, technical specifications, bid eligibility, foreign bidder rules, local-content requirements, licenses, tender fee, bond, tax, FX, payment terms, contract length, subcontracting permissions, source retrieval timestamp, amendments, contact channel, and evidence file hashes. Unknown values stay explicitly unknown.

## Entry decision flow
Discover → verify issuer and live notice → evaluate foreign eligibility → map local partner / direct bid → estimate compliance cost → obtain supplier quotes → model margins, FX and working capital → prepare bid/no-bid decision → submit only through official channel → monitor amendments and outcome.

## Release gates
- No unverified or stale tender in Live view.
- No invented contract value, eligibility, deadline or buyer contact.
- No project is marked investment-ready by simple presence on an official website.
- Keep a historical archive for expired notices, but hide them from default live search.
- Check country-specific restrictions and sanctions before advising actual entry.
