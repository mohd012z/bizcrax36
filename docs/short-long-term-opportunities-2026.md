# BizCrax36 — Short and Long Horizon Business Research (2026-10-27)

## Methodology
These are hypotheses for further customer and supplier validation, not live tenders, guaranteed returns or verified acquisition offers. Startup capital bands are illustrative planning assumptions. Score each candidate only after collecting original evidence, named prospective buyers, competitor prices, quotations, licenses, and unit economics.

## Research report gates
1. Source authenticity and freshness (issuer URL, timestamps, amendments).
2. Demand validation (interviews, procurement signals, signed expressions of interest).
3. Competitors, price benchmarking and defensible differentiation.
4. Technical and operating blueprint (people, tools, dependencies, licensing).
5. Unit economics, working capital, base/downside/upside cash flow and break-even.
6. Funding and partnership eligibility, with application deadlines.
7. Marketing channels, customer acquisition costs, sales cycle and 90-day test plan.
8. Risk register, stop-loss milestones and evidence quality assessment.

## Opportunity lifecycle
Research hypothesis → evidence collection → demand validated → commercial pilot → unit economics verified → scale review. Only live issuer-verified tender notices should appear in the separate Live Tender view. Evergreen business ideas must never be labelled live tenders.
